FULL STACK + AI COURSE

PHILOSOPHY OF
CRAFT LEARNING

This roadmap proposes a journey from manual software construction to advanced AI integration.

Under the premise of "craft learning", the goal is not simply to develop projects quickly, but to use AI to learn to code properly.

"Technology does not replace critical thinking; it acts as a multiplier of skills once the student has understood the technical fundamentals."

— Prof. Pelayo Méndez

General Objectives

01

Software Life Cycle: Understand from planning and design to deployment and maintenance.

02

Technical Base Mastery: Consolidate the manual use of semantic HTML, CSS, and interactive JavaScript.

03

AI as "Pair Programmer": Integrate Copilot to optimize workflow without losing technical autonomy.

04

Architectural Evolution: Transition from static applications to Full Stack and generative environments.

05

Avoid "Vibe Coding": Replace blind intuition with data-driven methodologies and clear structures.
